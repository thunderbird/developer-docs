# Design

### Bolt Design System & Design Contribution Process

When you contribute to the Thunderbird design, no matter which area you pick, we always ask that you follow the interface guidelines laid out in our [bolt design system](https://bolt.thunderbird.net/).

Then for your design contribution, the process will be roughly the same:

1. Choose a project. This could be an issue from our github repo of stand-alone defined design projects, or it could be an existing design issue in bugzilla.
   1. [design-contributions github repo](https://github.com/thunderbird/design-contributions/issues) - This is a carefully curated list of stand-alone design projects that need attention. You can filter by label to find which area might suit your skills and interest best.
   2. [existing design bugs](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/buglist.cgi?short_desc_type=allwordssubstr\&classification=Client%20Software\&classification=Developer%20Infrastructure\&classification=Components\&classification=Server%20Software\&classification=Other\&short_desc=design\&resolution=---\&query_format=advanced\&product=Chat%20Core\&product=MailNews%20Core\&product=Thunderbird\&list_id=18102399) - Here is a list of our open design bugs in Thunderbird.
2. Add a comment in the issue saying you'd like to work on this and ask any questions you have.
3. Provide design assets that meet the requirements.

### Types of Design Projects

There are several areas of design that come together to make Thunderbird the application you see and interact with today. Here are a few design areas where you can help out.

Note that these areas often overlap so don't worry about picking the "perfect" one.

#### Accessibility

We need help testing things like screen readers and braille keyboards, to make sure the application behaves in an intuitive way to integrate with these devices.&#x20;

Thunderbird is committed to digital accessibility and inclusion. Our goal is that people with diverse needs and abilities can use them with dignity, independence, and confidence.

We need help testing things like screen readers and braille keyboards, to make sure the application behaves in an intuitive way to integrate with these devices.

#### User Research

We periodically do user surveys to gather information on what users want most.

You can help us create targeted surveys to gather this data, and help shape our roadmap around what users actually want.

There are always UI tweaks needed to improve the visual experience of Thunderbird.

We conduct user research frequently to understand user needs. We always share opportunities to get involved on channels like [Topicbox UX](https://thunderbird.topicbox.com/groups/ux) and [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Thunderbird/).

#### User Interface & User Experience (UI/UX)

We are constantly refining Thunderbird's interface and design to build a smoother and modern experience. Help us spot polish opportunities, fix visual bugs, or streamline everyday workflows.

#### Content Design

The goal of the content design is to reduce confusion and improve clarity of the language we use in Thunderbird. We write in simple, clear English at about a Grade 8 reading level. We avoid technical terms when we can, while recognizing that some are necessary for accuracy.

Our goal is to make the source content easy to understand and easy to translate. We do not review or approve translations, since localizers decide what works best in their language and context. [Learn more about our content guidelines.](https://bolt.thunderbird.net/8b179dbfd/p/241b87-content-guidelines)
